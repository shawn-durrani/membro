Ledger
What is this & why it matters

Every fact ever kept about you — one card each. Cards are never thrown away: when something changes, the old card is flipped face-down (superseded) and kept as history. So you can always see what the AI believes now, what it used to believe, and where each belief came from.

Hold selected is for a card that turned out to be broken — a half-sentence, or a scrap that isn't really a fact about you. Tick it, say why, and it stops being used: it leaves recall and your profile and moves to Review, where you can put it back later with Approve. Nothing is deleted. Use Supersede instead when a card is simply out of date and a newer card replaces it.

Finding specific cards. The search box normally looks inside the card text. To look up cards by their id number instead, start with # — #1990 for one, #216,1394,1990 for several at once. That's how you act on a list of ids someone (or a report) handed you: paste them in, and every one appears together so you can tick them all and Hold them in a single go.

Passkeys
What is this & why it matters

Unlock with your fingerprint instead of typing your password. A passkey lets this browser unlock Membro with Touch ID (or Face ID on a phone) — quicker than the password, and nothing to remember or leak. Your password keeps working as the fallback, and the recovery secret still handles resets, so a passkey can never lock you out. One catch, by browser design: a passkey is tied to the exact web address it was made on, so enrol one here and another wherever else you open Membro (say, over your tailnet). The address 127.0.0.1 can't hold one at all — use localhost. Removing a passkey below stops it unlocking Membro; the copy on your device can then be tidied away in your device's password settings.

The vital signs of the file where all of this lives. If Integrity says ok and there's a recent backup, your memory is safe. Danger zone

The only real erasers in the app — and only you can press them. Everything else hides or retires things reversibly; these permanently destroy one fact or one stored file, with no undo and no history kept. Prefer Supersede (in the ledger) or Dismiss (in review) unless it should never have existed. Type the id and the word DELETE to enable each button. File ids are in the Files section above.
